design: larus-gl-2 v23 (data/designs/larus-gl-2.json), 1805.6 g, FROZEN (SPEC "Design freeze", "Support and containment":
v23 = v22 plus support and containment corrections). ROUND 15 (2026-11-03).
v22 assembled without clashes, but its packed parts floated at their mass positions, up to 80 mm above their trays. Its
forward tray stood 32.6 mm out of the nose, its boom clamps 22-27 mm out of the pod, and its pitot probe was not in the
check. v23 puts every packed part on the structure that carries it, through a stated catalogue fixing. Every enclosed
part stays 0.5 mm inside the loft, and the fittings left outside count in the pod's drag. v23 keeps the same parts, wing,
stroke, frequency law and controller laws. Every member and fixing is sized to its own loads by the existing rules.
Built official by src/design/round15.mjs --official. It refuses any clash, any unsupported part and any uncontained part
(officialRefusal; there is no override). Then came V5 on v23 itself, the calibration's fixed point, and the full gate
set, flown as round 14 flew v22 (missions.mjs --seeds 100 --corners, 1/3840 s, half-step check, 12 workers, measured
eta_s table).
The gates ran in the repo. Before every official run, git diff --stat sims/avis-shared/ showed this round's model.js and
the film agent's uncommitted bird.js, film.js, frame.js, live.js and materials.js. No file under src/ imports any of
those five, so the physics saw no film change.
The uniform eta_s 0.6 comparison ran in a scratch copy of the working tree, with 6 workers (out/sweep/round15/v23-eta0.6/).
The official run wrote out/flight/ (v23 logs, the files the gate records point to). Copies are in out/sweep/round15/flight/,
and v22's are in out/sweep/round14/flight/. This file and docs/status/final-verify.txt supersede the v22 versions.

What v23 changes (src/design/round15.mjs CARRIERS, mountsFor, CONTAINMENT, FIT_GRID; the packing in
sims/avis-shared/model.js packedSpecs, placeMounted, mountOrder; the carriers and fixings in src/design/airframe.js
carrierGeometry, af_carriers, motorBracketGeometry, af_motor, pitotTies; the checks in src/design/clearance.js
supportRecord, containmentRecord; the pod fit in src/design/fit.js):
  - Every packed part is mounted (params.layout.mounts): it rests flat on its carrier's top face at a stated x and z,
    never slid off it. Only the battery keeps its CG-trim x, sliding along x alone to the nearest place the loft holds
    it (12 mm aft of its lump, v22 20 mm).
  - Nose tray: a 6061-T6 channel (37.6 x 5 x 0.6 mm) at y -43.1 mm, from x 108.9 to 228 mm, with the battery centred on
    the pod's axis so it reaches furthest into the nose. It carries the ESC (across the tray), the airspeed board beside
    the receiver, and the battery. It stands on a riser: a 0.8 mm sheet web across the pod just forward of the crank
    stub, with two 7 mm flanges, an 8 mm foot screwed to the forward tray and an 8 mm tab under the nose tray.
  - Forward tray: now 38.4 x 7 x 0.8 mm at y -80 mm. It runs from the forward plate under the crank encoder to the
    riser, so its tip no longer reaches the nose (v22: 32.6 mm out of the loft).
  - Aft tray: 53.2 x 6 x 0.6 mm at y -81.2 mm, 123 mm long. It sits lower, to clear the coupler eyes at the bottom of the
    stroke. Fore to aft it carries the tail servos side by side, the flight controller beside the regulator, then the
    GPS. The regulator is now packed with the parts (its lump follows it), not after them.
  - Both trays sit inside their plates' outlines, so no plate post runs below them.
  - Ties: through slots in the carrier either side of each part. Each tie model is the smallest catalogue tie whose
    published bundle range takes the loop 2 (w + h + sheet): HellermannTyton T18R (to 69.1 mm) or T40R (to 125.7 mm).
    Counts: the battery and each servo 2, every other part 1, the pitot 2.
  - Crank encoder: the readhead hangs on the riser's web by the maker's own fixing (BRD01: 3 x M2 DIN 912 through its
    holes on the 34 mm circle, with spacers under 4 mm across and over 6 mm long). The holes are at 30, 150 and 270 deg,
    so every land lies on the web. The spacers are 3.8 / 2.2 mm 6061-T6 tube, 14 mm long, with M2 nuts on the web's front.
    The readhead stands 2.5 mm, not 0.5 mm, forward of stage 1's teeth (params.layout.encoder_gap_mm), because the
    screw heads on its back must clear the DS1-60. The crank stub is 2 mm longer. New catalogue entries:
    screw-iso4762-m2x25-8.8 (0.795 g; Sp 580 MPa x As 2.07 mm2 = 1200.6 N) and nut-iso4032-m2-8 (0.149 g).
  - Motor: the maker's drawing (Scorpion SII-3008 full dimension, read 2026-11-03, out/downloads/scorpion-sii-3008/)
    puts its 4 x M3 threads on the back (stator) face, 25 mm across, and the page lists a cross-style back-plate mount.
    Its front is the rotating bell. v22's fixing, M3 into the front face on the forward plate, does not exist.
    In v23 the motor hangs by its back face on a 2 mm 6061-T6 bracket: the hull of the four screw lands, with a sleeve
    bonded on each shoulder rail. The rails now carry that force: the larger lever-rule share of the motor's inertia and
    first mesh force, plus its torque and prying as couples over the rails. The bell stays 1.9 mm from the forward
    plate, so it clears the rail's 1.4 mm lock flange by 0.5 mm, as in v22. The forward-plate motor doubler is gone.
  - Plate faces: the boom clamps, trays and motor gap are measured from the plates' own faces (1.2 / 1.6 mm), not the
    nominal 2 mm plate (params.layout.plate_faces 'actual'). v22's clamps stood 0.4 / 0.2 mm off their plates.
  - Pitot: the probe lies along the nose tray's right flange, outside it, its bottom flush with the base, from x 196 mm
    for its 100 mm, on 2 T18R ties at x 200 and 207.5 mm. It leaves the nose through its own hole. In v22 it floated
    30 mm above the nose tip, outside the loft.
  - Pod: the fit rule now holds every enclosed part, and the drag counts the fittings left outside. The fit's grid
    starts at v22's 0.11 x 0.14 m and steps the centre line by 1 mm (FIT_GRID). It picks 0.12 x 0.14 m at y -0.027 m.
    At 0.11 m wide, the stage 3 wheel (0.478 mm by the 96-point outline) and the shoulder housings (under 0.1 mm) are
    too close to the skin at every centre line. At 0.11 x 0.15, the wheel is.

The checks (src/design/clearance.js; pinned in test/design/clearance-builders.test.js, test/design/round15.test.js and
test/design/clearance.test.js):
  - Support (structure.clearance.support). For each packed part, the check names the carrier, the contact face and the
    fixing, and measures the gap from the part to that face. The gap must be zero within 0.001 mm: touching. The pair
    check proves separately that the part and carrier never interpenetrate, because joined pairs are checked at zero
    gap. A part resting on a tray must also have its centre of mass over the contact patch and every tie on the carrier.
    The motor is checked on its bracket, the encoder actuator in its bore, the readhead on the riser through its
    spacers, and the pitot against its flange.
  - Containment (structure.clearance.containment). Every solid of the assembly is checked against the loft: the frame,
    the drive, the packed boxes, their ties and the fixings, the moving ones at 72 crank angles at each of three
    amplitudes. It uses model.js's elliptic measure, which understates the true distance. A solid of a declared opening
    may cross the skin, and the window it crosses is recorded. An outside fitting must stay 0.5 mm clear of the skin.
    The material outside the loft is projected on the (z, y) plane, so a shielded part counts once. That frontal area,
    at Cd 1.2, is added to the pod's frontal drag (body.CdA_front_outside_m2, round11.mjs outsideDrag), before the trim.
  - The body fit (fit.js) now holds every enclosed solid and clears every outside fitting, so the pod is sized to them.
  - Ties: each one is drawn as four slabs round the part and its carrier's sheet. The slots they pass through are not
    drawn.
