design: larus-gl-2 v24 (data/designs/larus-gl-2.json), 1749.6 g, FROZEN (SPEC "Design freeze", "Completeness and assembly
order": v24 = v23 plus completeness and assembly corrections). ROUND 16 (2026-11-03).
v23 assembled without clashes, every packed part on its carrier and inside the pod, but the check held only 76 parts. Of the
BOM's physical lines, the screws and nuts, the keys, the wing's spars, rods, ribs, torque rods and twist servos, the tail's
rods and ribs, the formers and five of the ties had no solids at all. Their counts and sizes were never compared with
anything drawn, and nothing showed the bird could be put together. v24 draws every physical BOM line, tags each solid with
its line, matches counts and sizes, states the assembly sequence and checks it, and fixes what those checks found. It keeps
the same wing planform, span and section, stroke amplitude, frequency law, controller laws, motor, pack and gears.
Built official by src/design/round16.mjs --official. It refuses any clash, any unsupported or uncontained part, any physical
BOM line without its solids or with a wrong count or size, and any sequence step that passes through a placed part or a
solid in no step (officialRefusal; there is no override). Then V5 ran on v24 itself, the calibration came to its fixed
point, and the full gate set was flown as round 15 flew v23 (missions.mjs --seeds 100 --corners, 1/3840 s, half-step
check, 12 workers, measured eta_s table).
The gates ran in the repo. Before the official run, git diff --stat sims/avis-shared/ showed only this round's model.js
(placeMounted). No film file had changed, and no file under src/ imports one.

The checks (new in round 16; pinned in test/design/round16.test.js, test/design/round16-official.test.js and the updated
test/design/clearance-builders.test.js and clearance.test.js):
  - Completeness (src/design/completeness.js, structure.clearance.completeness). Each solid of the assembly carries its BOM
    line (p.bom). Bought parts are keyed by catalogue id and summed across lines; structure lines by name. For every
    physical line: the number of distinct items drawn must equal the BOM's count, and each item's size must match: catalogue
    dimensions within 0.05 mm (a rounding tolerance; gears: tip circle, bore, face; journals: bore, OD, length, flange;
    motor: can and shaft; fasteners: head, shank, length, nut across flats and height, tie band), stock within 0.5 mm of
    length per item (spars, rods, boom, probe), and everything else by volume within 10%. A part drawn as its envelope (a
    lightened rib, a former's band) must hold at least the BOM's volume (one-sided). Every solid must be tagged and every tag
    must name a line. Exempt, each with its reason in the record: the wing, tail and fuselage covering films (no fixed place
    as a solid: the lifting surfaces the flight model flies, and the loft the containment check keeps every part inside).
    Not BOM lines, also stated: glue (Araldite 2011, LOCTITE 406 with SF 770, LOCTITE 638), wire, and the 15% margin.
  - Holes (clearance.js holeRecord, structure.clearance.holes). The THROUGH and THREAD exemptions let a screw's shank pass
    the parts it clamps. So that the exemption cannot hide a misplaced screw, every shank is checked to lie in metal of
    each part it passes: the hole's whole circle inside the part's section, where the part spans the shank's x (a
    vertical screw: its square across x and z). Edge distances stay the bolt checks' own (airframe.js). v24: 44 holes,
    all in.
  - Assembly order (src/design/sequence.js, params.layout.sequence, structure.clearance.assembly). The design states its
    sequence: 83 steps, each one part or a sub-assembly put together first away from the bird, with the straight line it
    arrives along and how far out it starts. The drive is held at phase 0, nominal amplitude. A step along x is checked
    exactly (a prism moving along x sweeps its own section over the union of its x ranges). A step across x is checked by
    conservative advancement: from each position the step advances by the true gap to the nearest placed solid, never more
    than 0.125 mm and never less than 0.1 mm, so no 0.5 mm wall can be jumped. Touching is allowed, sub-assemblies are
    allowed, and the pairs the clearance check exempts (a key in its keyway, a shank in its hole, gears in mesh, a rod
    through its rib, a tube in another's bore) may meet. Screws go in along their own axis from the head's side, nuts on
    from the tip's side. A cable tie is threaded round its bundle and closed in place, so a threaded step does not slide:
    it must come after every part it binds. Every solid must be in exactly one step. Tests refuse a toy impossible order, a
    tie before its bundle, the v24 order with the forward plate moved ahead of the gear shafts, and the v23 one-piece crank.

What the checks found in v23, and how v24 fixes each (src/design/round16.mjs ROUND16, SEQUENCE, CONTAINMENT; fixings.js;
wing-solids.js; the geometry in airframe.js, linkage.js, build.js and clearance.js):
  - Screws and nuts had no place. v24 lays out every one (fixings.js fixingLayout): axis, head face, the parts it clamps
    head side first, nut face, and length. Each is the shortest catalogue screw of the thread that takes the stack plus a
    full nut, or 1.5 d of thread when tapped. The BOM now counts them by length from that layout: 16 M3 x 8, 4 M3 x 12 and
    16 M3 nuts. v23 counted 28 screws and 24 nuts.
  - The rails' torsion-lock lands on the forward plate sat inside the motor can's footprint and under the stage-1 wheel. No
    land there could take a screw head or nut. The aft seat boss could not sit on either face of the aft plate: on the
    forward face the forward bracket's sleeve was in the way, and on the aft face the rocker hub overlapped it by about 1.9 mm.
    v24 moves each lock to the aft plate's aft face (lock_at 'aft outer'). Each lock has two screws, 26 mm from the rail at
    -63 and -39 deg, below and outboard of it, where head, nut, shank and flange reach nothing (out/sweep/round16/scripts/
    landsolve.mjs). The heads are inside the box on the plate's front face, and an ear of the plate surrounds them. The
    bosses are gone. With two lands the seat needs more bearing, so the aft plate is 2.0 mm (was 1.6). At the v23 shoulder
    span the thicker plate brought the forward shoulder bushings' flanges and housing counterbores to 0.10 mm from it (4
    clashes; probe out/sweep/round16/probes/span366.log, quick16span.mjs), so the shoulder span is 37.4 mm (was 36.6).
  - The tail's roots were at y 0.02 m, but the boom is at 0.062 m. The boom solid ended at x -0.9458 m, while the tail root
    chord runs to -1.0888 m. v24 puts the roots at the boom's height (tailY_m = boom y). The boom runs to 5 mm aft of the
    tail spar. Running it to the trailing edge moved the CG 45 mm aft.
  - The boom clamps' screws, at a 14 mm pitch, stood outside the 13 mm clamp block. v24 adds a 26 x 7.5 mm screw flange
    under each collar, with the plates' pads rounded on the screws. The plates' edge circles are now rounded on the trays'
    screws. Clamp stack: 6 mm clamp plus the plate is over 8 mm with the nut, so these screws are M3 x 12.
  - The floor's forward flange blocked the crank wheel's way in, so the floor goes on after the crank shaft. The aft tray's
    screw pitch is now 38 mm.
  - Former 1 at x -100 mm met the GPS and the aft tray. Formers are now 4 mm bands, 2 mm thick, round the design's own loft
    at x -100, -30, 97, 165 and 200 mm. Each has a tab tied to the tray above it, and they are built on the final loft at
    check time. Their line follows their drawn volume: 2.13 g. v23's 12.6 g was a nominal "50% lightened" estimate.
  - Keys (6 lines) are drawn in their keyways (KEYWAY). Wing lines are drawn by wing-solids.js: inner spar from the socket
    base to the elbow, outer spar and torque rod to the station where the leading edge rod stops clearing the spar (s
    0.9383 m), LE and TE rods in 10 mm pieces along the chord line, 20 ribs as their envelopes from the first station clear
    of the loft through the stroke (s 0.052 m; v23's film put rib 1 at the hinge, inside the pod), twist servos. The tail
    is drawn the same way. Its v23 line (film, spar and ribs in one) is split into tail film, tail rods (4) and tail ribs (8).
  - The crank (see "The crank" below). Also: the readhead's fixing split into its solids; a hole in the riser web so the
    encoder's actuator passes it onto the stub; the coupler bushings' flanges with counterbores in the eyes; the gearbox
    panels drawn with their end flanges where each plate's face is free; the shoulder bracket arms at their drawn length
    from the bushing to the rail (8.6 mm; v23's BOM said 21.1 mm to the housings).

The crank. v23's crank was one titanium piece: shaft, web and pin. Its shaft must go in from forward through the aft
plate's bushing before the forward plate, and then the web meets the aft bushing by 13 mm, its flange by 7.5 mm, the aft
plate by 9.5 mm and the aft boss by 13 mm (test 'v24-like: the v23 one-piece crank ... cannot go in'). v24 splits it.
The shaft (with its 15 mm wheel seat) goes in from forward through the aft bushing before the forward plate. The web and
pin are one Ti-6Al-4V part, keyed on the shaft's aft stub with a DIN 6885 3 x 3 x 6 mm Ti-6Al-4V key in a 1.8 mm
keyseat. They go on from aft after the shaft and before the couplers. The key carries the torque and is rated by the
keyMember rules. The web is held axially with LOCTITE 638 on its bore. LOCTITE publishes 638's strength on steel and
aluminium only, not titanium, so that axial fixing is unrated. M7 reports it as RC practice with the crank's peak torque
(see the RC-practice list for the scope question).
Mass: shaft 31.67 g, web and pin 9.59 g, key 0.24 g, 41.50 g in all (v23 one piece 40.51 g).

A mass error, found by M6 and fixed before the gates counted. The first official v24 (1803.2 g, gated at 14:01) failed
M6: its BOM summed to 1749.6 g but its mass model to 1803.2 g. Round 16 brings the keel-and-boom and formers lines with
the airframe's items, so their lengths and areas follow their solids. build.js still summed every airframe item into the
gearbox's lump, while those two lines also have lumps of their own: 46.55 g, x 1.15 margin = 53.5 g, was counted twice.

solids'). Everything below is the corrected v24. The calibration loop and every gate were re-run on it. The flawed run is
kept in the scratchpad for the record, and none of its numbers are used here.

    crank arm on a shaft. Its torque goes through a rated key, and only its axial retention is the unrated LOCTITE 638
    bond. Whether that falls inside the ruling is the operator's call. If not, a rated axial fixing would replace the
    bond, for example an M3 screw and washer into the stub's end, rated as a fastener in tension.
