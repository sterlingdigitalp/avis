design: larus-gl-2 v22 (data/designs/larus-gl-2.json), 1761.9 g, FROZEN (SPEC "Design freeze", "Assembly clearance":
v22 = v21 plus clearance corrections). ROUND 14 (2026-11-03). v21's layout did not assemble: the film found four drive
clashes, and the clearance agent's v22 candidate still had 47 clashing part pairs. v22 is laid out again under the SPEC's
layout freedom. It keeps every v21 part except the encoder actuator, and the same wing, stroke, frequency law and
controller laws. Every member is sized to its own loads by the existing rules.
Built official by src/design/round14.mjs --official. The clearance check passes: zero clashes through the full stroke and
against the pod. Then come V5 on v22 itself, the calibration's fixed point, and the full gate set, flown as freeze
verification flew v21 (missions.mjs --seeds 100 --corners, 1/3840 s, half-step check, 12 workers, measured eta_s table).
The gates ran in the repo. Before every official run, git diff --stat sims/avis-shared/ showed only this round's model.js.
The uniform eta_s 0.6 comparison ran in a scratch copy of the working tree, with 6 workers
(out/sweep/round14/v22-eta0.6/).

What v22 changes (src/design/round14.mjs ROUND14.set; the model in sims/avis-shared/model.js clearancePlanes, linkage.js
rockerHub, clearance.js):
  - Horn stacking. The two horns pass within 11 mm of each other, so the two stacks sit at different x. The left boss is
    aft of its eye and the right boss forward of its eye. Each boss is 0.5 mm from its eye. The horn pins end flush with
    the eyes. The bosses are 7 mm long (v21: 8 mm, a 1 mm gap, pins 1 mm proud).
  - Shoulder spacing 36.6 mm (v21: 24 mm), so both stacks sit between the housings. An eye or boss overlaps a housing
    radially by up to 2.4 mm, so it cannot share the housing's x. This is v21's clash 2, and it is cleared by stacking,
    not by resizing.
  - The crank web is 8.7 mm forward of the hinge (v21: 1.5 mm). Both coupler eyes sit on the crank pin, 0.5 mm apart and
    0.5 mm aft of the web. The aft plate is 0.5 mm past the crank bushing's flange. This is the same crank overhang as
    the clearance agent's candidate, the tightest the eyes allow.
  - The rocker hub is turned down to 13.6 mm where the coupler eyes pass. Elsewhere it keeps 14.5 mm and its 1.2 d0 edge
    distance. It ends 0.5 mm aft of the aft plate. A 9 mm collar on the shoulder pin keeps the forward axial stop,
    0.5 mm short of the forward bushing's flange.
  - The rails are 20.75 mm below and 4 mm outboard of the hinges (v21: 11.5 mm above, in the spar sockets' sweep). They
    are 13/8 mm (v21: 12/8). Their seats are 31.5 mm, which gives 1.2 d0 edge distance. The bracket arms are 11 mm wide
    (v21: 10). The rail's torsion lock flange (1.4 mm) is at the forward plate's aft face, with 6 M3 screws on a 15 mm
    arc from 40 to 172 degrees (v21: 3 screws, aft plate).
  - Stage 1 overhangs forward of the forward plate, and the motor sits inside the box, screwed to the forward plate's aft
    face. The reason: gear shaft 2 is 29 mm from gear shaft 3, inside the DS1-60's 31 mm tip circle. No shaft that spans
    the plates can pass stage 1's plane, whatever the fold. The fold is now stated (links 157.8, 48.7, 1.3, 110.5 deg),
    chosen with the real check against every solid.
  - The encoder. The catalogue's 8 mm actuator cannot go on the 10 mm crank shaft. It is replaced by the maker's 10 mm
    BA100 (RLS BRD01: ID 10 H7, OD 22, H 9.5 mm, 5.7 g, 3 x M3 DIN 913 included; read by the BR10 ordered with magnet type
    12). It sits on a stub of the crank shaft, forward of stage 1: readhead x 86.0-92.9 mm, actuator 96.9-106.4 mm, ride
    height 4 mm.
    The encoder was tried inside the box first. That lengthened the box, the gears' CG moved forward, and the fit put the
    battery 71 mm further aft. The pod's nose shortened until no pod in the fit grid held the gears.
  - Frame routing. The side panels are at z +-45 mm, y -20 to 9 mm, 0.9 mm thick (v21: z +-31 at hinge height). The
    horizontal panel (still named "top panel", the film reads that name) is a floor at y -81 mm, 24 mm wide (v21: y 56.5,
    touching the boom). Both trays are at y -84 mm, under the crank eyes' sweep and the encoder (v21: -30 and -76). The
    plate posts are 16 mm wide (v21: 12), because the posts to the boom are longer now.
  - The pod follows from the fit rule, which now keeps every packed part, gear tip circle and the motor can 0.5 mm inside
    the loft. Under the clearance rule there is no 0.5 mm protrusion tolerance (model.js layoutParts, fit.js protrusions).
    The pod is 0.11 x 0.14 m, centre -0.03 m (v21: 0.09 x 0.13, -0.02). The battery's lump is 207.7 mm (v21: 256.5).
  Catalogue: sensor-rls-orbis-actuator-10mm added; the BR10 readhead entry gains its drawn dimensions. The covering entry
  film-oracover-air-light now holds the one real product: ORACOVER Air Light light scale white 331-099 (-002, -010),
  colour "light scale white". Lanitz's technical data sheet gives ca. 17 g/m2 and 12 um for Air Indoor and Air Light
  together, and the TDS figure is used, so the film mass is unchanged at 0 g. The 2010 maker catalogue's "approx. 18" is
  recorded in the entry. At 18 g/m2 the film would weigh +1.9 g.

The check, checked (src/design/clearance.js; every fix pinned in test/design/clearance.test.js):
  - Joined parts were exempt. They are now checked at zero gap: they may touch but never interpenetrate. Gears in mesh are
    still exempt. This exposed modelling errors, now fixed: housings had no counterbore for the bushing flange; bracket
    arms (capsules) reached into the rail and the bushing bore; panels ran to the plates' mid-planes; the boom clamp
    blocks were not bored.
  - The forward bracket's sleeve was assumed centred on its housing, which put it through the plate where the rail is
    seated. It now starts 0.5 mm clear of the plate.
  - The plates had no hole for the boom, which passes both by design. They now have a 0.5 mm clearance hole.
  - Pieces that were not checked and now are: plate posts and pads, the rail lock flange, the encoder, and the servo
    regulator, packed as the film packs it.
  - Gear tip circles were reported against the pod but never counted, and the motor was not reported at all. Both now
    count.
  - Packed parts now keep the running clearance to each other and to the drive box (it had been 0.1 mm).
  - Information only, outside every rule: some frame and drive parts stand outside the pod loft. The worst are the boom
    (it leaves the tail), the forward tray (32.6 mm, its tip below the nose taper), the boom clamps and pads (22-27 mm,
    the boom runs at y 62 mm over a 40 mm pod top), the spar sockets (20.9 mm, the wing roots), and the horn eyes (7.5 mm
    at the top of the stroke). v21 had the same kind: boom 62, sockets 22.1, clamps 16.7, aft tray 10.4, lock flange
    9.3 mm. Script: out/sweep/round14/scripts/podframe.mjs.
