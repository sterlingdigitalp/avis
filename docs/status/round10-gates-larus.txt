Item 5 (w2): at 1/1920 s, w2 moved -10.8% at half step (v19, seed 72). Diagnosis (S/r10/w2trace*.log, w2diff*.log): the
  peak is a 4.3 rad/s roll at t = 54 s; it moves 6.6% under a 1e-9 m start offset because the governor's quantised
  encoder, voltage and current readings make the closed loop discontinuous; with quantisation removed the offset changes
  nothing but the half step still moves it, and state differences halve with each halving of the step (calm air, no
  noise): the flight model converges at FIRST order. Source: aerodynamic internal states advanced once per step (the
  strip lifting line takes one frozen-Jacobian Newton step per flight step; the Wagner lag states; the wind held over the
  step). No stiff spring or contact was found. At 1/3840 s the population max moves 2.9% (v19) and 1.1% (v20) at half
  step, so the whole gate set moves to 1/3840 s (about twice the run time). Fixing the scheme (iterating the lifting
  line to convergence each step) would change the V5-calibrated model and was not attempted this round.
