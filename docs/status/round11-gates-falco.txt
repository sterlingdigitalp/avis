design: falco-pf-2 (Falco PF-2, 1.0-1.2 m span band). NOT REBUILT: no round-11 build converged, so
data/designs/falco-pf-2.json stays at v2 (round 2, built under round-2 rules) and no Falco gate file was written.
src/design/round11.mjs refuses to write a design whose free-flight trim did not converge (SPEC ruling after round 1:
"A trim that does not converge is reported as a failure, never as a result"), and missions.mjs needs the converged
controller table to fly anything. Every number below is a build or probe measurement, not a gate file.

Rules applied (the round-10 rule set, unchanged, through src/design/round11.mjs --design falco over round 10's
pipeline on the Larus v18 base, which carries the rule parameters): rated linkage and airframe members with the M7
bearing and edge-distance rules; KHK gear ratings under their stated conditions (JGMA KV on the accuracy row, reversing
load, "weak shaft" KH-beta) and the train search (scratchpad r11/trainsearch11.mjs, every module-1 KHK gear in the
catalogue); RC-practice shaft-to-hub fixings within their published figures; controller at 240 Hz with the 20 ms delay
and TECS; flight step 1/3840 s; 15% margin; catalogue parts only; span inside 1.0-1.2 m (round11.mjs checkSpan).
Round 11's two model fixes apply: the E387 negative stall at the measured -6.3 deg, and bushings judged on their
supported length.

Result: NO Falco design trims. Two failure families:
  - Low wing loading (AR 3-3.5, S 0.41-0.48 m2, 1220-1434 g, WL 29-33 N/m2): the static trim at 10-11 m/s asks
    8.4 Hz at 11 deg body pitch, above every rated envelope (4.3-4.8 Hz: motor current, motor voltage on 2S, gear
    rating or twist-servo torque, whichever binds); in free flight at the envelope's top the bird sinks 1.0-1.3 m/s
    at 10-11 m/s and 90-120 W, and the trims at 10-12 m/s diverge (altitude leaves +-30 m within 25 s, rolling to
    20-70 deg). Closest of this family: AR 3.5, C-2808-22, 6 N m train (DS1-18/30, SSG1-15/DS1-60,
    MSGA1-18/MSGB1-45, ratio 16.67), 1220 g, WL 29.1: sinks 1.04 m/s at 11.1 m/s, 4.83 Hz (motor continuous current),
    107 W.
  - High aspect ratio, fast (AR 4.5-5.5, S 0.26-0.32 m2, 1160-1310 g, WL 36-48 N/m2): free flight comes closest to
    level at 12.4-14.7 m/s (sink 0.25-0.6 m/s at the envelope's top, 106-118 W), but never converges. Closest: AR 5.5,
    C-2808-22, 9 N m train (DS1-20/32, MSGA1-18/DS1-50, MSGA1-18/MSGB1-40 x2, ratio 21.95), 1292 g, WL 48.4: sinks
    0.25 m/s at 14.7 m/s, 5.62 Hz, 118 W. At WL 36-48 and rho 1.00 kg/m3 the 1 g stall speed is 7.7-9.0 m/s at
    CL 1.2, so an 8 m/s release (M1) and a 9 m/s touchdown (M5) are out of reach whatever the drive.
  Where the mass goes (closest AR 5.5 build, g): drive linkage and gearbox plates 317, gears 285, margin 169,
  servos 123 (4 x MKS HV6160, the lightest eligible on the 2S pack), battery 104, airframe 91, wing and tail 86,
  motor 81, avionics 38. Larus v20 for comparison: 326, 371, 208, 123, 104, 122, 166, 140, 38 (1598 g on 0.667 m2).
  The fixed items (servos, battery, avionics, the drive's linkage sized by the Larus base) do not shrink with the
  wing, so Falco carries 73-90% of Larus's mass on 39-72% of its area.

Verdict: Falco PF-2 does not close under the current rules. It fails earlier than Larus: no catalogue-part Falco
holds level flight within its ratings, so no mission gate can even be flown.
